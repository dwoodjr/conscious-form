Water dissolves. Sugar vanishes into brine. Clay disperses. Salt surrenders its structure to the slow work of liquid. Did Sun Ra Fear Cryosleep? captures these dissolution events through DIY hydrophones — piezo membranes pressed against the surface of things ending — and transforms them into granular clouds of synthesis, each texture a kind of speculative memory: what is preserved, what is lost, and who gets to decide. The work holds Sun Ra's Afrofuturist refusal of death alongside the thermodynamic fact that nothing is ever truly gone — only changed state. Presented at the Society for Neuroscience and Creativity, FringeArts, Philadelphia (June 2026) and the Co-Creativity in Music, Sound, and AI symposium at the Culver Center of the Arts, UC Riverside (June 2026).
