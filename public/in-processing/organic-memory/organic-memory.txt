Organic Memory Triptych is a series of three interactive sonic sculptures that together constitute a research-creation inquiry into distributed creative intelligence, bodily knowledge, and the material life of sound. Each movement approaches sonicity from a different register — spatial, temporal, and haptic — enacting a practice the work names speculocultural technopoiesis: a speculative, culturally rooted making in which the technological object is never neutral but always already carries memory, relation, and care. Together, the sculptures ask what it might mean to listen with history, to make with matter, and to refuse the separation of the sensory from the political.
