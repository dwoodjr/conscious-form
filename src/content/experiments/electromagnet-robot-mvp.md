Minimum viable prototype for an electromagnet-driven robotic system developed as part of an Artist in Residence with the [Robotics, Automation, and Dance (RAD) Lab](https://radlab.dance/) in Philadelphia.

ESP32 microcontroller feeding a 4-channel MOSFET driver board, switching a set of cylindrical electromagnets in choreographed sequences. Initial test: magnetic field interactions with conductive and ferromagnetic fabric — exploring how the robot can manipulate soft materials and structure movement without direct physical contact.
