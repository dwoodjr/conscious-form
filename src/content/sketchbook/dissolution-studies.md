Granular synthesis built from hydrophone recordings of objects dissolving: sugar, salt, clay, wax in water. Each material yields a distinct texture — the physics of dissolution as a scoring parameter. These studies feed directly into *Did Sun Ra Fear Cryosleep?*

## Process

Piezo contact microphones pressed against vessels during dissolution. Recordings captured at 96kHz, then sliced into grain clouds in Max/MSP. Grain size, scatter, and playback rate modulated by the real-time amplitude envelope of the dissolving source.
