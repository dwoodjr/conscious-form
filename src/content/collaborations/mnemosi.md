## Overview

*Mnemosi* was one installation in *Womb: The Black Wealth Project*, an exhibition by Maria Bauman (MBDance), Drexel's Rankin Scholar, at the university's Pearlstein Gallery. The concept for *Mnemosi* was mine. Maria gave creative input and shaped its direction through the exhibition as a whole.

Visitors were invited to leave a message of abundance and growth. Each message fed Mnemosi, who grew over the course of the exhibition into a young Black girl.

## Concept and approach

One low-barrier gesture, leaving a message, is the whole interaction. My challenge was making that input feel consequential to someone with no technical knowledge. I designed the audiovisual response so that every contribution changed the state of the piece where the visitor could see and hear it, tying each person's action to Mnemosi's growth. The layout led visitors up to the piece to contribute, then back to watch Mnemosi change.

Real-time audio processing and generative visuals made up the system, along with a custom pipeline I wrote to capture each message and transform it. I handled the concept, spatial layout, interaction model, audio engineering and all of the technical development.
