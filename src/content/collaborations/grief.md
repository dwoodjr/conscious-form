## Overview

*Gr[i]ef* is a staged dance performance by the Drexel Dance Ensemble, presented at Mandell Theater as part of Drexel University Performing Arts' 50th Year Celebration. My part was the visual scenography: animation and simulation projected across the stage for the length of the piece.

## How I built it

Every image on stage started as motion capture of a dancer. From that capture I built the simulations and animation in Houdini, so the forms on screen follow a real dancer's movement. I rendered the sequences out of Houdini and timed them to the audio as fixed playback. The theater had no live tracking or real-time system; everything the audience saw was rendered ahead of the performance.
