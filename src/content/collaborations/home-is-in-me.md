## Overview

*Home is in Me* is a staged dance performance by the Colorado Conservatory of Dance in Denver. Sandra Park, the conservatory's director, commissioned the visuals: animated 3D environments that served as the performance's scenography.

## Who did what

Arefeh Ahmadi and I co-created the visuals. Arefeh handled textures, color design and image editing. The animation was mine, along with every asset and environment: I modeled the assets in Houdini and built and animated the environments in Unreal Engine. The conservatory supplied the music tracks and audio clips, and I composited the final video and audio in DaVinci Resolve and rendered the deliverable for the stage.
